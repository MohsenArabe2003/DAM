AFTER-SCHOOL DRAMA CLUBأنشطة ما بعد المدرسة

After-School Drama Club

After school hours, Dar Al Masrah offers structured drama classes that build skills, teach self-confidence, and help your child rediscover their values, culture and identity — in Arabic.

★ See schools enrolled in the programme

A typical session

Warm-up games to shake the school day off → scene work and story games in Arabic → rehearsal of this season's original play, written by Sahar for this exact group.

What's included

Two sessions every week, all materials and costumes, and a final original production performed for family and friends at the end of the season.

BECOME A TRAINERانضم لنا

Become a Trainer

Love the stage? Love kids more? Trainers are the cast behind the cast — the people who turn a Sunday afternoon into a rehearsal kids refuse to miss.

Who we look for

A background in theatre, performance, or teaching. Comfortable leading a room of 7–15 year-olds. Fluent, warm Arabic. Patience that survives twenty kids in costume.

What you get

Paid seasonal contracts, training directly with Sahar Bahrawi, real production credits every season, and a front-row seat to kids finding their voice.

Apply now

Your application opens in WhatsApp — nothing is sent until you press send there.
